# Refund Policy

## Standard Refund Window

Refunds are not available after 14 days from purchase.

## Exceptions

Refund requests may be reviewed when:

- Major service outage
- Billing errors
- Compliance-related issues

## Retention Offers

Support teams may offer:

- Service credits
- Temporary discounts
- Account upgrades

## Churn Prevention

Customers threatening cancellation should be escalated to the retention team.
