# Pricing Policy

## Plans

### Standard Plan
- $99/month
- Up to 20 users

### Pro Plan
- $299/month
- Up to 100 users

### Enterprise Plan
- Custom pricing
- Dedicated support

## Discounts

### Non-Profit Discount
- 30% discount on Standard Plan

## Billing

### Pro-Rata Upgrades
When upgrading mid-cycle, customers are charged only for the remaining days in the billing period.

### Enterprise Pricing
Enterprise customers receive custom contracts and pricing based on seat count and requirements.
