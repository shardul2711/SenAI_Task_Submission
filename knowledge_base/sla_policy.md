# SLA Policy

## Uptime Commitment

- Guaranteed uptime: 99.9%

## Incident Response

### P0 Incident
- Initial response: 15 minutes

### P1 Incident
- Initial response: 1 hour

### P2 Incident
- Initial response: 4 hours

## RCA

Root Cause Analysis reports for P0 incidents must be delivered within 24 hours.

## Service Credits

Customers affected by SLA violations may receive service credits according to contract terms.
